# TrainBud example prompts

Use after setup (`/trainbud:trainbud-setup` + restart).

## Single-topic

- What did I do today?
- Show me all my runs from last week.
- How's my sleep been this week?
- Is my resting heart rate trending better?
- Am I recovered enough to train hard today?
- How stressed have I been this week?
- What's my VO2 max trend over the last month?

## Compound (best results)

- I played tennis yesterday — how was my sleep after, and am I recovered today?
- Give me a weekly training summary: activities, sleep average, recovery trend.
- I'm planning a hard run tomorrow — based on sleep and recovery, is that a good idea?
- Am I overtraining? Look at my activities, sleep, stress, and recovery together.
- Summarize my fitness this week in plain English — workouts, sleep, HR, and weight.

## The ones Connect cannot answer

These use the tools the plugin's skill previously did not mention, and they are
the reason to have TrainBud rather than the Connect app open in a tab.

- Is anything unusual in my data right now, compared with my own baselines?
- How did my last run compare with the other times I ran that distance?
- Was that session faster or slower than usual, and was my heart rate higher?
- Give me this week against last week, with the load forecast.
- Remember that my marathon is on 2026-11-08 — keep it in mind when I ask about training.
- That run felt like an 8 out of 10 and my calves were sore; log it.
- What do you have on record about my goals and races?
